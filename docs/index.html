epi_int_3d animations

Animations of simulations made with epi_int_3d, a streamline N-body code for planetary rings, with play, pause and a frame slider.

A spiral bending wave

A satellite's m = 2 vertical resonance launches a spiral bending wave in a self-gravitating ring; the test and its comparison with linear theory are in bending_waves.ipynb.

  • The ring's height, face-on and unrolled: grayscale, white above the plane and black below, in the frame rotating with the pattern; face-on with the ring's width stretched radially, and unrolled, longitude across and distance from resonance up.
  • The ring's height, streamline by streamline: every 3rd streamline's height versus longitude, each offset by its distance from resonance.

A spiral density wave

The same ring, with a satellite's m = 2 inner Lindblad resonance in its inner part, launches a spiral density wave; the test is in density_waves.ipynb.

  • The ring's surface density, face-on and unrolled: white dense, black sparse, in the frame rotating with the pattern; face-on with the ring's width stretched radially, and unrolled, longitude across and distance from resonance up, which shows the tightly wound spiral's slope.

The test notebooks

Each test of the paper's Section 4, executed, with its comparisons with theory; the paper's ledger links each claim to its section.

  • bending_waves: the spiral bending wave in a self-gravitating ring, tests 1–8.
  • vertical_resonance: massless particles at a satellite's vertical resonance.
  • lindblad_resonance: massless particles at a satellite's inner Lindblad resonance, test 9.
  • density_waves: the spiral density wave in a self-gravitating ring, tests 10–11.
  • inclined: inclined test particles, the vertical motion of Section 2.
